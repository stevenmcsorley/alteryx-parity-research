# Responsible research and intellectual-property boundary

This project targets independently written, lawful functional alternatives. It does not grant authority to inspect every third-party product or to redistribute proprietary content.

- Alteryx's EULA includes restrictions on reverse engineering and on using its software to develop competing products. Before any REA investigation of Alteryx-specific EXE/DLL/assembly code, confirm legal entitlement and authorisation; a trial or ordinary user licence is not assumed sufficient.
- UK CDPA sections 50BA and 50B provide limited rights around observing a lawful copy and interoperability decompilation, subject to conditions. They are not a general permission to duplicate a competitor's implementation. Obtain legal review for applicability and contract restrictions.
- Public functional descriptions, independent math/standards implementations and own tests are preferred. Do not copy original source, decompiled output, protected icons, branding, documentation wholesale, secrets, paid datasets or licensed modules into this project.
- No circumventing licensing/DRM, account restrictions, authentication, network access controls, rate limits, or other technical protection. No unauthorized network interception.
- Developer-generated reference fixtures must be original or appropriately licensed; label data provenance and rights.
- Separate research notes from implementation work to support independent creation and licence review. Keep findings limited to what may be lawfully recorded and used.
- Everything published must pass a third-party IP, data protection, contract and export review before commercial distribution.
